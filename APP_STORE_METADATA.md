## Description

```
Take a photo of your fridge. Get dinner.

The Fridge & Cupboard looks at what is already on your shelves and gives you
real meals you can cook tonight — no meal plan to build, no shopping trip, no
recipe search. Your first fridge scan and your first cupboard scan are free, and
you do not need an account or a card to try them.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Tap Scan My Fridge and take a photo (or pick one from your photos).
2. See everything that was spotted, with what is fresh and what to use first.
3. Get meal ideas built from those exact ingredients, with steps you can follow.

WHAT ELSE IS IN THERE
• Scan your cupboard for pantry-first meals from rice, pasta, cans and spices
• Use My Leftovers — turn last night's dinner into something new
• Use It Soon — cook the food that is about to go bad before it does
• Store Mode — photograph a shelf at the store for ripeness and storage tips
• Shopping list grouped by aisle, ready to text, email or print
• Save, share, email, text or print any recipe
• Talk to Chef Super J hands-free while you cook, or just type
• Cooking mode with big readable steps and timers

MADE FOR REAL KITCHENS
Simple, large-text screens for seniors and caregivers. Soft-bite, low-energy,
one-pot, low-sodium and heart-healthy options. Dietary filters including vegan,
vegetarian, gluten-free, diabetic-friendly, GERD and no-citric-acid. Recipes in
your language.

BUILT BY A CHEF
Created by career chef and brain tumor survivor Justin "Super J" Banta, to help
families spend less on groceries, throw less food away, and eat better tonight.

EVERY FEATURE IS AVAILABLE
Nothing is sold inside this iOS version. There are no prices, purchase buttons,
or links to an outside payment page.

Privacy Policy: https://thefridgeandcupboard.com/privacy
Terms of Use: https://thefridgeandcupboard.com/terms
Support: support@thefridgeandcupboard.com
```
